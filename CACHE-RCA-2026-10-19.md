Explanation: mover is scheduled, not automatically triggered by fullness. Minimum free space controls placement of newly created files; it does not reserve physical space or relocate files already growing on cache. The reserve is smaller than several media files, so an already-started large write can consume the remaining space. This is a supported mechanism and a strong explanation for the observed exhaustion; the exact file-open/free-space sequence was not captured.

Official reference: https://docs.unraid.net/unraid-os/using-unraid-to/manage-storage/shares/
