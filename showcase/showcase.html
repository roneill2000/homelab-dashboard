01 · Overview

Overview

The top card says "All clear" or lists what needs attention, and each item links to its page. Pinned services come next, then the quick actions: wake or sleep the gaming PC, pause DNS blocking for five minutes, and check for updates.

On a phoneThe health card and four pinned services fit on the first screen.
ConfirmationsActions that change something ask first, and the server only accepts them from the dashboard.
Missing readingsEach reading has a timestamp. If a source stops answering, its tile shows a dash instead of zero.

02 · Design

Material 3 Expressive design

The colours come from Google's Material colour library, generated from a single dark green for both the light and dark themes. Layout, shapes and motion follow Material 3 Expressive, Google's 2025 update to Material Design.

ShapesThe health icon sits in a scalloped shape that changes when something is wrong. Each service category has its own icon shape, and the loading indicator morphs between shapes.
MotionPages slide into place, a tapped row turns into the next page's title, and buttons change shape when pressed. The system's Reduce motion setting turns all of it off.
AccessibilityText contrast is at least 4.5:1 in both themes. Automated tests cover keyboard focus, dialogs and navigation.

03 · Storage & network

Storage and network

Storage opens with each drive's free space, live read and write rates and SMART health. Network opens with the latest speed test and the two Pi-hole servers. History charts come after that, one disk or host at a time on a phone or side by side on a wider screen.

Cache forecastEstimates when the Unraid cache will fill and starts the mover before it does.
Chart coloursEach line colour was checked for colour-blind readers in both themes.
GapsIf a host reboots, its chart shows a gap for the missing time.

05 · Recovery

Backups and recovery

Local Proxmox backups, the off-site copy in Google Drive and Unraid parity are shown together, with restore test results below them. Each night after the backups finish, the A310 model reviews the host warning logs.

Off-site copyrestic encrypts photos, Proxmox backups, Unraid app data and router and host configs before uploading them to Google Drive. It keeps 7 daily, 4 weekly and 12 monthly snapshots.
Restore testsA scheduled test downloads sample files from Google Drive and compares them with the originals.
Section linksButtons at the top of the page jump to recovery, logs, the change history or the service list.

06 · Updates

Updates

Each Proxmox container is snapshotted before apt upgrade runs, and Docker apps update through Unraid's own updater. Guests update one at a time; a failure stops the run and shows the rollback command. Items with an update waiting are listed first, and current or skipped ones are folded away.

07 · Fleet & services

Hosts, containers and services

Proxmox, Unraid and the Orange Pi are at the top, followed by CPU, memory and state for every VM, container and Docker app. The services page groups everything by category, and unreachable, stale and unmonitored services are labelled in text as well as colour.
